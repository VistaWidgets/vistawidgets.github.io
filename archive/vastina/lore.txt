VASTINA
(a.k.a. Long Horn Assistant L.H.A.)

Vastina is a scrapped "Helper" Office Assistent, meant to be much more advanced than Clippy, she was made on October 17th, 2005, and was scrapped in late 2006 because she wouldn't have been finished by the time Windows Vista released. Because of this, she never got actual visuals -- in their place, she uses the Vista Desktop Widgets. After years, the PC she was on turned up on Ebay, and was bought by the owner of the Twitter account.

Age: 41
Height: 5'4
Her Developer/Creator: Jayson Johnston
Twitter/Current PC Owner: Unnamed/No Name.
